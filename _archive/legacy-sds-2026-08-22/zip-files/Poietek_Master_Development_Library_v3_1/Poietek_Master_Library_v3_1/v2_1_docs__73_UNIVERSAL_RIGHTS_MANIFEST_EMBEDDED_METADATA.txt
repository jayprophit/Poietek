73 — UNIVERSAL RIGHTS MANIFEST & DOCUMENT-LIKE EMBEDDED METADATA
================================================================

COMPANION
schemas/universal-rights-manifest.schema.v1.json

APPLIES TO

audio
video
images
samples
presets
plugins
skins/themes
clips
templates
documents
other creator content.

THREE DISTINCT LAYERS

CREDIT
Who contributed.

RIGHT
Who owns/administers/licences what.

PAYMENT
Who receives money under a transaction/agreement.

Do not automatically give every contributor ownership or payment simply because
they are credited.

DOCUMENT-LIKE INSPECTOR

Selecting a file can show:

File properties
Creation date/time
Content hash
Author/creator
Credits
Rights
Identifiers
Licence
Society registrations
Provenance
Commerce policy
Payment beneficiaries
Version/history.

EMBEDDING

Project standard metadata for common tools.
Use media-specific standard tags.
Use C2PA Content Credentials for signed provenance where supported.
Use DDEX structures for relevant music-industry exchanges.
Embed a compact/public Poietek rights projection where compatibility is proven.
Keep the complete canonical manifest separately by content hash.

WHY EXTERNAL CANONICAL COPY

Transcoders/distributors can strip unknown metadata.
A file must not be the sole copy of a legal/rights record.

PRIVACY

Never embed:
banking info
tax data
private contracts
OAuth tokens
private society credentials
private addresses.

ACCEPTANCE
META-001 standard media remains valid.
META-002 common metadata remains readable.
META-003 Poietek renders complete rights document.
META-004 public embed excludes private fields.
META-005 manifest version is linked to exact content hash.
