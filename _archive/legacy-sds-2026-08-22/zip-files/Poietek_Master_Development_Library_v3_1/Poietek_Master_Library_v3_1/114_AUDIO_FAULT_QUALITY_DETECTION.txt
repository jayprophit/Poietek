114 — AUDIO FAULT & QUALITY DETECTION
=====================================

CHECKS

HARD CLIPPING
samples at/over representable digital limit.

NEAR CLIPPING
frequent peaks too close to ceiling.

TRUE-PEAK RISK
inter-sample peak risk from standards analyzer.

DC OFFSET
non-zero mean.

POLARITY / PHASE
negative correlation
channel polarity anomalies
mono compatibility.

NOISE
broadband noise floor
hiss
room/air-conditioning
preamp noise.

HUM
50/60 Hz family plus harmonics.
Region-aware but detect rather than assume.

DROPOUT
unexpected digital silence/discontinuity during active programme.

CLICK/POP
transient anomaly detection.

SIBILANCE
spectral/context detector.

PLOSIVE
low-frequency transient detector.

RUMBLE
low-frequency persistent energy.

HARSHNESS / RESONANCE
advisory spectral features, not universal artistic faults.

STEREO
left/right imbalance
excessive anti-correlation
unexpected mono channel.

SPECTRAL

AI can compare spectral features against:
the same project's reference tracks
user-chosen reference
source-class ranges.

Never say:
"Correct EQ is X".

Say:
"Compared with your reference, 2–5 kHz is 4 dB more prominent."
