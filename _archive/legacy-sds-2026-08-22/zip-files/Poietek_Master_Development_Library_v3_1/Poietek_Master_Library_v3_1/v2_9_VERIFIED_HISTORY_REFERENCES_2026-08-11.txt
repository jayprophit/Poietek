Verified/current references used 11 August 2026:

ISO 16:1975
Official ISO page:
https://www.iso.org/standard/3601.html
Specifies A=440 Hz. Published 1975; reviewed and confirmed in 2022.

Nature, 27 May 1939
G. W. C. Kaye, "International Standard of Concert Pitch"
Documents the International Conference of 11–12 May 1939 held at Broadcasting
House under the International Standards Association, with delegates from France,
Germany, Great Britain, Holland and Italy plus views/representation from other
standards/broadcast bodies.

Max Planck Institute for the History of Science
"Tuning the World"
Describes A440 as the result of decades of international negotiation among
performers, composers, instrument builders, scientists and state actors.

No credible evidence was found in this review supporting a claim that the current
A440 standard was changed by the Rothschild family "a few years ago".
