Official sources reviewed 11 August 2026:

PRS for Music:
- Online member account sign-up uses a membership number (CAE/IPI).
- Nexus exposes recording ISRC, work ISWC, PRS Tunecode, and writer IPI data.
- Registered PRS works receive a unique tune code.

PPL:
- recording rightsholder members register new recordings;
- performers claim performances on recordings already in PPL repertoire.

The MLC:
- eligible members can register individual works, bulk works, or CWR;
- writer IPI can be used in writer search/registration workflows.

SoundExchange:
- creators/rights owners register and manage repertoire/royalty information;
- official materials describe ISRC-rich repertoire submissions and DDEX/flat-file routes.

DDEX:
- RIN captures project/session/contributor/recording/work data and supports
  proprietary identifiers by namespace;
- RIN can link messages to audio using URI/hash;
- RDR-N exchanges recording/music-video contributor and rights-claim data;
- production implementations require an implementation licence.

C2PA:
- current Content Credentials specification is 2.4 and is designed for
  signed media provenance/history.

Stripe Connect:
- separate charges and transfers can split a platform payment across multiple
  connected accounts, subject to geographic/processor rules.

Apple / Google Play:
- current policies govern digital goods, creator content, tips/gifts and
  alternative billing by storefront/territory. Commerce must be policy-routed.
