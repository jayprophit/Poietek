164 — VIDEO PLAYER TUNING & AUDIO/VIDEO SYNC
============================================

VIDEO DURATION MUST STAY FIXED.

If the community video player's audio is retuned:
pitch changes
tempo/duration remain fixed.

This preserves:
lip sync
cuts
captions
chapters
timecode-aligned metadata.

Do not change audio playback speed from 432 -> 440 for a normal music video.

SPECIAL CASE

If a creator intentionally wants:
slower/faster visual playback with pitch,
that is a separate creative playback mode.

PLAYER PIPELINE

Video decode
+
audio decode
-> tuned audio DSP
-> common media clock
-> output.

SYNC MONITOR

Measure:
audio/video drift
buffer underrun
retune processor latency.

Apply latency compensation.

END DOCUMENT 164
