85 — SETTLEMENT MERKLE ROOTS & PAYMENT/ROYALTY PROOFS
=====================================================

COMPANION
packages/blockchain/src/SettlementMerkle.ts
database/blockchain-anchor-ledger.v1.sql

PROBLEM

Thousands of payment/royalty lines are expensive and privacy-invasive to store
directly on chain.

SOLUTION

Keep private settlement lines in Poietek's normal ledger.
Create a Merkle tree over canonical allocation records.
Anchor only the Merkle root.

Each contributor can receive:
their settlement line
Merkle inclusion proof
batch anchor receipt.

This can prove the contributor's line was included in the anchored batch without
publishing every contributor's private payment details.

COVERED SOURCES

Poietek marketplace sales
tips/support
licensing
imported PRS/PPL/MLC/SoundExchange statements
distribution statements
other royalty statements.

IMPORTANT

For an external royalty statement, Poietek anchors what the statement says.
It does not make the blockchain the payer or change the society's legal/payment
process.

FIAT PAYMENT

Payment processor/bank receipt remains off-chain.
Hash receipt into settlement evidence.

ON-CHAIN PAYMENT

Future optional mode may settle eligible transactions with tokenized funds or
stable-value rails, but requires legal, tax, custody, sanctions and smart-contract
audit review before production.

ACCEPTANCE
SET-001 altered settlement line fails inclusion proof.
SET-002 private line not publicly readable.
SET-003 source statement hash retained.
SET-004 fiat processor receipt linked by hash.
