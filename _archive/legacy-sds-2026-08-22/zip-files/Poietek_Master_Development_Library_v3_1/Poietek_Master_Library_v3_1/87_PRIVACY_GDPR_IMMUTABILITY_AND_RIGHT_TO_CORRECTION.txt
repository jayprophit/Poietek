87 — PRIVACY, IMMUTABILITY & RIGHTS CORRECTION
==============================================

BLOCKCHAINS ARE HARD TO ERASE.

Therefore never write:
legal name unless specifically public/appropriate
email
home address
tax ID
bank details
private society login/member secret
private contracts
unreleased lyrics/audio
private messages.

USE HASHES/COMMITMENTS.

RIGHT TO CORRECTION

Blockchain record isn't edited.

Example:
Manifest v1 anchored.
Contributor spelling/split later corrected.
Create Manifest v2.
All required people approve v2.
Anchor v2.
Record v1 -> superseded by v2.

The history remains auditable while current application truth points to v2.

HASH PRIVACY

Naively hashing low-entropy personal data can still leak via guessing.

Use:
random/salted identifiers
commitments
or avoid anchoring personal fields entirely.

ACCEPTANCE
PRIVCHAIN-001 delete account can remove off-chain PII.
PRIVCHAIN-002 public chain contains no raw email.
PRIVCHAIN-003 correction generates new version.
PRIVCHAIN-004 UI clearly shows current vs historical manifest.
