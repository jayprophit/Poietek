JOIN OPTIONS

1 Contributor scans project QR on their own phone/tablet/laptop.
2 Contributor opens invite link/email.
3 Producer searches an existing Poietek profile.
4 Contributor creates a guest identity on the studio device.
5 Guest identity is claimed later from their own device/account.

OWNER / ADMIN CONTROLS

Role.
Permissions.
Access mode.
Expiry.
Concurrent sessions.
Devices per contributor.
Project-only or reusable team membership.
Revoke device.
Suspend contributor.
Allow/deny export.
Allow/deny rights editing.
Allow/deny financial visibility.

ACCESS MODES

ONE_SESSION
Valid for the current production session.

TIME_LIMITED
Ends at specified time.

PROJECT_LIFETIME
Access continues for this project.

TEAM_REUSABLE
Contributor can join future projects in the same team when invited/allowed.

ORGANISATION
Company/studio account policy.

UNLIMITED
No project-level device/session cap, subject to system/security/product plan.

IMPORTANT

"Unlimited" does not mean unauthenticated.
Every active device/session still has identity, token, audit and revocation.

STUDIO-DEVICE GUEST

Contributor enters:
display name
email/phone optional
role
society IDs optional.

Poietek creates:
Temporary Party
Session contribution record.

Later:
Claim this contribution
-> authenticate on contributor's own device
-> prove invite/claim token
-> merge temporary Party into permanent Party
-> preserve original audit history.

CREDITS SURVIVE ACCESS REVOCATION

Removing access must never remove:
credits
approved rights
session history
registration evidence.

FINALIZATION

When a recording is selected as final:
Poietek asks contributors with unresolved metadata/rights to review.
Remote contributors receive review tasks.
Owner cannot silently impersonate another contributor's approval.
