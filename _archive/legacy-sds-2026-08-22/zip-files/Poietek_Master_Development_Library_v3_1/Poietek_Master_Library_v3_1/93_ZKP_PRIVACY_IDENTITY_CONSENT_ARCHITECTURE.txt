93 — ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PRIVACY, IDENTITY & CONSENT
===============================================

ZKP candidate uses:
prove verified contributor membership without revealing raw society member ID;
prove required seller/KYC state without exposing the identity document;
prove inclusion in an approved settlement without revealing unrelated finances;
prove age/eligibility threshold where lawful and useful.

Every ZK feature requires:
precise statement;
public inputs;
private witness;
proof-system selection;
setup/trust analysis;
performance benchmark;
privacy/threat model;
independent cryptographic review.

ZKP does not replace:
legal agreements;
KYC rules;
society registrations;
tax;
payout compliance;
normal access control.

Verified Contributor Credential.
