Primary/current references reviewed 11 August 2026:

ISO 16:1975
https://www.iso.org/standard/3601.html
Standard musical pitch: A on treble stave = 440 Hz, accuracy 0.5 Hz.
ISO page says confirmed in 2022 and current.

ITU-R BS.1770
https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770/en
BS.1770-5 (11/2023) is current/in force for programme loudness and true-peak
measurement algorithms.

EBU R128 v5
https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128
Current page recommends average programme loudness -23 LUFS plus Loudness Range
and Maximum True Peak descriptors.

ATSC A/85:2026-07
https://www.atsc.org/atsc-documents/a85-techniques-for-establishing-and-maintaining-audio-loudness-for-digital-television/
Approved 8 July 2026. Current recommended practice for audio loudness in digital
television and hybrid broadcast/streaming workflows.
