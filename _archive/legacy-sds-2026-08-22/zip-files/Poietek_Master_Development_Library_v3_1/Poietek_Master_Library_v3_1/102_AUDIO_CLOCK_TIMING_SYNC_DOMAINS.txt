102 — AUDIO CLOCK, TIMING & SYNC DOMAINS
========================================

SAMPLE CLOCK

Digital audio devices must agree on the sampling frequency or be isolated through
sample-rate conversion.

Poietek models explicit Clock Domains.

ONE LEADER PER SYNCHRONOUS DOMAIN.

Possible sources:
internal
Word Clock
Dante PTP
AES67 PTPv2
MADI embedded
ADAT embedded
AES3 embedded
AES50 embedded
external house clock.

DANTE

Current Dante documentation states that devices use IEEE 1588 PTP and synchronize
to a leader clock with sample-accurate network alignment.

AES67/ST 2110 interoperation can use PTPv2 where supported.

MADI / OPTICAL

Optical is a physical medium, not one protocol.

Optical connection may be:
MADI fibre
ADAT optical
other optical transport.

Poietek identifies the protocol, not merely "optical cable."

MADI is a point-to-point multichannel digital audio interface and may run over
coaxial or fibre; real interfaces can clock from MADI, Word Clock or a network
clock.

AES50

AES50 defines bidirectional multichannel digital audio over Category-5-style
cabling with audio synchronization carried with the connection.

USB

USB audio clock behavior is adapter/device-specific.
Poietek treats the selected native/Web audio device backend as an endpoint and
reports actual clock/lock capabilities.

SRC

If two domains cannot share clock, an explicit sample-rate-conversion bridge may
connect them.

TRANSPORT IS NOT SAMPLE CLOCK

MIDI Clock/MTC/MMC/timecode controls do not replace the audio sampling clock.
