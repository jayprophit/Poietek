CURRENT EXAMPLE

Spotify's artist guidance currently states:
Normal playback normalization: -14 LUFS.
Recommended master True Peak: below -1 dBTP.
For masters louder than -14 LUFS, guidance recommends below -2 dBTP to reduce
additional encoding distortion.

This is a DELIVERY PREVIEW, not a rule that every artistic master must be exactly
-14 LUFS.

APPLE

Apple Digital Masters emphasizes delivering high-quality source masters and
provides mastering/audition tools that can detect peaks/clipping after AAC
encoding.

Poietek should invoke/replicate only documented compatible checks and should not
invent an Apple loudness target where Apple has not stated one.
