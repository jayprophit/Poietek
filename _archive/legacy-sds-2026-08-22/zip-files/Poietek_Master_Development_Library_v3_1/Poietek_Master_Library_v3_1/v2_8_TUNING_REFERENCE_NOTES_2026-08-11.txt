Primary/current sources reviewed 11 August 2026:

ISO 16:1975:
https://www.iso.org/standard/3601.html
ISO specifies standard musical pitch A=440 Hz and lists this edition as confirmed
in 2022/current.

The MIDI Association:
https://midi.org/microtuning-and-alternative-intonation-systems
Official MIDI.org material describes the MIDI Tuning Standard and Scala SCL/KBM
microtuning interoperability.

MIDI 2.0:
Current MIDI Association developer material states MIDI 2.0 preserves MIDI Tuning
Standard concepts and adds per-note pitch/tuning mechanisms.

432 Hz research examples:
PubMed PMID 31031095:
Music Tuned to 440 Hz Versus 432 Hz and the Health Effects: double-blind crossover
pilot, 33 participants. Authors explicitly described the evidence gap and called
for larger trials.

PubMed PMID 35545982:
432 Hz vs 440 Hz randomized pilot in emergency nurses.

PubMed PMID 40061232:
2025 double-blind crossover study in kickboxers reported performance/response
differences but did not establish a universal health or "natural frequency"
standard.

Poietek policy:
Support A432 as creative/research option without presenting universal healing or
biological claims as settled science.
