PRS/MCPS

PRS assigns a unique tune code to registered works, and Nexus exposes links among
work/recording metadata such as tunecode, ISWC, ISRC and writer IPI.
Poietek should store these returned identifiers against the work/recording.

PPL

New recordings are registered by recording-rightsholder members; performers claim
performances on existing PPL repertoire. Poietek routes users according to their
capacity rather than submitting the wrong claim type.

THE MLC

Member workflows currently support individual, bulk and CWR registration routes.

SOUNDEXCHANGE

SoundExchange Direct lets creators/rights owners search/claim/manage repertoire;
SoundExchange also documents DDEX and flat-file repertoire submissions for
registered rights owners.
