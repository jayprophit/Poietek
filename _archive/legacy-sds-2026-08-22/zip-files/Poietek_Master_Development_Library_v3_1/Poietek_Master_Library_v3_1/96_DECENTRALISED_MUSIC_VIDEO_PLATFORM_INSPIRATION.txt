REFERENCE PRINCIPLE

Study current public architectures and workflows; do not copy proprietary assets
or assume token economics are required.

analytics;

LBRY/ODYSEE INSPIRATION

Useful:

P2P content delivery;
desktop/mobile/web clients;
free or priced content;
tips/support;
channel/content syncing;
content address/distribution distinct from user-facing site.

POIETEK ADAPTATION:
optional P2P replicas;
creator-owned export;
provider-independent channel identity;
cross-platform sync;
one-click external channel import/export where APIs permit.

YOUTUBE INSPIRATION

Useful centralized product patterns:
channels;
playlists;
subscriptions;
comments;
search;
uploads;
captions;
analytics/reporting;
creator/content-owner dashboards.

POIETEK ADAPTATION:
modern creator UX without requiring central ownership of the canonical media.

WHAT NOT TO COPY

Forced-wallet onboarding.
Speculative token reward loops.
Public-by-default finances.
Unmoderated illegal/infringing distribution.
One blockchain as the only index.
One provider as the only content host.
