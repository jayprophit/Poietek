145 — HARMONIC SERIES & JUST INTONATION
=======================================

This is the strongest technical meaning for an "organic harmonic" mode.

Natural acoustic resonators generate harmonic/partial relationships.

Just intonation uses intervals represented by simple frequency ratios.

Examples:
Unison       1/1
Octave       2/1
Perfect fifth 3/2
Perfect fourth 4/3
Major third  5/4
Minor third  6/5.

Poietek can:

show ratios
show cents difference from 12-TET
retune synth/MIDI notes
generate reference tones
analyse chord beating
preview adaptive tuning.

LIMITATION

Fixed just intonation is key/context dependent.

A ratio ideal for one harmony can become awkward after modulation.

ADAPTIVE JUST

Detect chord/context.
Choose bounded rational offsets.
Protect melodic continuity.
Avoid sudden pitch jumps.
Keep user-set maximum retune cents.

This becomes a Poietek creative engine, not a universal correctness rule.
