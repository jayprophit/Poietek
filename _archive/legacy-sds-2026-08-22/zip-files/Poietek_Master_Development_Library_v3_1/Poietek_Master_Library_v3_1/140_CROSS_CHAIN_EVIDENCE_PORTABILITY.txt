140 — CROSS-CHAIN EVIDENCE PORTABILITY
======================================

COMPANION
packages/chains/src/ChainEvidenceAdapter.ts

CHAIN SUPPORT IS OPTIONAL.

Poietek may anchor:
Rights Manifest hash
approval root
settlement root
release snapshot.

Different adapters may support:
EVM networks
Bitcoin evidence formats
other reviewed ledgers.

CANONICAL EVIDENCE

Poietek Evidence Receipt is provider-independent.

CHAIN SWITCH

Future evidence can use another network.
Old receipts remain verifiable against their original network.

NO DEFAULT CROSS-CHAIN BRIDGE

Moving tokens/funds between chains is not required for creative interoperability
and introduces materially higher security risk.

Keep payment-bridge functionality separate and disabled by default.
