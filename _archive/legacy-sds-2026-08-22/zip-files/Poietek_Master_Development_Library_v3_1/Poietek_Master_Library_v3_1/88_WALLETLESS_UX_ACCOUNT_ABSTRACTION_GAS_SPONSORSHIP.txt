88 — WALLETLESS UX, SMART ACCOUNTS & GAS SPONSORSHIP
=====================================================

PRODUCT RULE

Users should not need to understand:
seed phrases
gas
chain IDs
RPCs
wallet networks

to record a song or receive ordinary royalties.

LEVEL 0
No blockchain account.
Poietek operates off-chain/local.

LEVEL 1
Poietek anchors evidence on behalf of user/service.
User sees verification receipt.

LEVEL 2
User links an existing wallet if desired.

LEVEL 3
Smart-account/account-abstraction UX.
Programmable recovery, batching and sponsored gas may improve experience.

Ethereum's current account-abstraction direction explicitly supports smart-contract
wallets and can enable applications/paymasters to sponsor transaction fees.

KEY RECOVERY

Never make a contributor lose rights records because a wallet key is lost.

Poietek Party identity remains canonical.
Wallets are linked proof/signing methods that can be rotated under audited policy.

ACCEPTANCE
WALLET-001 ordinary user can ignore blockchain.
WALLET-002 lost wallet does not delete rights record.
WALLET-003 wallet rotation audited.
WALLET-004 sponsored gas cost tracked by platform.
