150 — TUNING AUTOMATION & REGIONAL MAPS
=======================================

COMPANION
schemas/session-tuning-map.schema.v1.json

Most projects use one tuning.

Advanced projects can change tuning by region.

Examples:

Intro:
A432 / just.

Verse:
A432 / 12-TET.

Bridge:
19-EDO.

Outro:
adaptive just.

MIDI instruments can follow region maps when supported.

AUDIO

Existing recordings do not automatically retune when crossing a map boundary.
Audio operation must be explicit.

LIVE

Region tuning changes must be preloaded and validated for connected hardware.

NO SURPRISE PITCH JUMPS

Live Mode can lock tuning profile against accidental edits.
