POIETEK / STUDIO DAW STATION (SDS)
MASTER DEVELOPMENT LIBRARY — VERSION 1.0

Compiled from:
1) The complete product-development conversation supplied by the creator.
2) Deep review of GitHub repository: jayprophit/Studio-Daw-Station-SDS-
3) Current cross-platform/offline-first architecture recommendations.

WORKING PRODUCT NAME
Poietek is the working ecosystem name discussed in the design conversation.
Studio DAW Station (SDS) is the current repository/product label.
Final public branding should be chosen only after trademark/domain checks.

CORE RULE
One app. One project model. One creative graph. Many workspaces.
Offline first. Cloud optional. Hardware agnostic. Collaboration capable.
Professional depth without forcing beginners to see every advanced control.

LEGAL / CLEAN-ROOM RULE
Competitor products, patents, papers, hardware and interfaces may be studied for
functional principles, workflows and interoperability requirements. Do not copy
proprietary source code, protected assets, branded UI expression, undocumented
confidential protocols, or patented claims without appropriate legal review or
licensing. Compatibility layers should use documented/open interfaces wherever
possible.

===============================================================================

STANDARD PROFESSIONAL CORE
Projects, templates, audio/MIDI/instrument tracks, folders, groups, buses, VCAs,
sends, sidechains, recording, comping, editing, automation, tempo maps, mixer,
plugins, freeze/bounce, stems, undo, autosave, shortcuts, drag/drop and rendering.

SAMPLER / CHOP
Waveform/spectrogram/turntable views.
Manual, lazy, transient, equal, beat/bar, silence, AI and zero-crossing chops.
Per-slice start/end, loop, pitch, tune, gain, pan, reverse, fade, filter,
envelope, stretch and choke.
Hardware banks are export profiles, not app limits.

STEMS
Vocals/drums/bass/music as compatibility baseline, optional finer models.
Linked chop markers across stems, stem mixer and stem-to-pad mapping.

PAD BUILDER
Logical pad grid, classification, velocity layers, round robin, choke, gate/loop,
root notes, filters, envelopes, colors and hardware adaptation.

DRUM LAB
Step grid, velocity, probability, ratchets, flam, ghost notes, microtiming,
note repeat, swing, groove, polymeter, Euclidean/conditional tools.

GROOVE
Extract timing/velocity from audio/MIDI, apply by percentage and save templates.

PIANO ROLL
Ghost notes, chord stamps, scale highlighting, MPE/per-note expression,
microtiming, probability, articulations, strum/flam/arp/humanize.

SCORE
Staff/percussion notation, lyrics, chord symbols, articulations, tuplets, tab and
MusicXML-style interchange where supported. Keep score and MIDI synchronized.

HARMONY
Global chord track, key/scale maps, chord pads, scale assistant, detection,
substitution, transpose and alternative tunings.

SESSION PLAYERS
Editable drummer/bassist/keys/guitar/percussion/strings/brass performances
following chord track and user-defined complexity/groove.

CLIP/SCENE
Launch clips/patterns/stems and record performance into Arrangement.

ARRANGEMENT
Real audio/MIDI/video ruler, split/trim/slip/fade/stretch/loop/consolidate,
sections, markers and alternate scratch area.

RETROSPECTIVE CAPTURE
MIDI high priority. Optional rolling audio with privacy/memory controls.

VOCAL/NOTE AUDIO
Monophonic pitch/timing first: pitch curve, drift, vibrato, formant, timing,
sibilance and gain. Polyphonic editing later research target.

ALIGNMENT
Guide vs doubles: timing and optional pitch, loose through tight.

SPECTRAL/REPAIR
Spectrogram, selection, harmonic tools, heal/attenuate, de-click, de-hum,
de-noise, de-clip, de-reverb, de-bleed and voice isolation.

SOUND DESIGN
Sampler, synths, granular/wavetable later, modulation, resampling and macros.

AUTOMATION
Audio, MIDI, plugins, mixer, tempo, video and show-control parameters.

MIX
Buses, groups, parallel containers, snapshots, track versions, reference tracks,
gain-matched A/B, metering and phase/stereo/loudness tools.

MASTER
EQ/dynamic EQ, compression/multiband, saturation, image, clipper, limiter,
resonance control, LUFS/true peak, reference assistance.

EXPORT
Audio master, stems, loops, samples, MIDI, kit/program packages and project
archive. Settings for rate, depth, dither, range/tails and realtime/offline.
Device-specific exports only where verified.
