86 — ON-CHAIN VS OFF-CHAIN PAYMENT ROUTING
==========================================

PAYMENT ROUTER

Transaction can use:
Stripe Connect
Apple payment rail
Google Play Billing
bank transfer
PayPal/other marketplace processor
future compliant on-chain rail.

RIGHTS GRAPH stays independent of payment rail.

OFF-CHAIN FIAT

Best early choice for ordinary marketplace users.

Benefits:
card/bank UX
refunds/chargebacks
KYC/payout infrastructure
ordinary currency.

Blockchain:
anchor settlement receipts/roots only.

ON-CHAIN

Optional later for:
web3-native marketplace items
public grants
creator collectives
machine-to-machine licensing
transparent community pools.

Do not force crypto wallets on ordinary artists/listeners.

CUSTODY

Poietek should avoid holding user funds directly where a regulated processor can
handle them.

If a smart contract holds money:
independent audit
reentrancy review
emergency/pause policy
upgrade policy
lost-key recovery design
sanctions/AML/tax/legal review
must precede release.

ACCEPTANCE
PAY-001 rights split same regardless of payment rail.
PAY-002 fiat user never needs wallet.
PAY-003 blockchain unavailable does not block card sale.
PAY-004 on-chain payout feature disabled until audit/legal gate.
