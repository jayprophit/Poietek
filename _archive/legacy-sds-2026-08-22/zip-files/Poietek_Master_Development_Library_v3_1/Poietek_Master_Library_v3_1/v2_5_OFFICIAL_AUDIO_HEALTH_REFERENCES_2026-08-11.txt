Official/current sources reviewed 11 August 2026:

ITU-R BS.1770-5:
https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770/en
Current in-force recommendation (approved November 2023) for algorithms to measure
audio programme loudness and true-peak audio level.

EBU R128 v5:
https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128
https://tech.ebu.ch/loudness/
Programme loudness recommendation and EBU Mode ecosystem. Current public EBU
material describes Momentary (400 ms), Short Term (3 s), Integrated and Loudness
Range/True Peak concepts; EBU supplies a loudness test set/reference pink-noise
material.

Spotify artist loudness guidance:
https://support.spotify.com/artists/article/loudness-normalization/
Current page states Normal playback normalization at -14 LUFS following ITU 1770,
with mastering guidance below -1 dBTP; louder-than--14-LUFS masters are advised to
stay below -2 dBTP to reduce encoding distortion.

Apple Digital Masters:
https://www.apple.com/uk/apple-music/apple-digital-masters/
Apple provides high-quality-master guidance and mastering/audition tools for
testing AAC encoding, peaks and clipping.

Apple MusicUnderstanding LoudnessResult:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/musicunderstanding/loudnessresult
Apple documents integrated, momentary, short-term and peak loudness results aligned
with LUFS/ITU-R BS.1770.
