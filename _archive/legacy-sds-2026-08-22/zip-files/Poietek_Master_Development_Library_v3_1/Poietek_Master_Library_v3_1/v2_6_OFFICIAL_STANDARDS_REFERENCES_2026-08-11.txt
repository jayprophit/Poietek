Primary/current references reviewed 11 August 2026:

ITU-R BT.709-6:
https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BT.709/en
In-force HDTV production/programme-exchange parameter recommendation.

ITU-R BT.2100-3:
https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BT.2100
In force from February 2025 for HDR television production/international exchange.

SMPTE standards overview / D-Cinema:
https://www.smpte.org/standards/overview
https://www.smpte.org/blog/understanding-standards-digital-cinema-format
Current D-Cinema suites include SMPTE 428, 429, 430, 431, 432 and 433.

SMPTE ST 2067 IMF:
https://www.smpte.org/standards/st2067
File-based interoperable masters for global/multi-platform delivery.

SMPTE ST 2110:
https://www.smpte.org/standards/st2110
Professional video/audio/data over managed IP.

EBU R128:
https://tech.ebu.ch/loudness/
R128 v5 family includes broadcast, streaming, radio and cinematic supplements.

W3C WebVTT:
https://www.w3.org/TR/webvtt/all/
https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/
Current Candidate Recommendation Draft published 20 May 2026; WebVTT is widely
used for web captions/subtitles.

MusicBrainz genres:
https://musicbrainz.org/doc/Genre
https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_API
https://musicbrainz.org/genres
MusicBrainz treats genres as part of the tag system and notes that genres are
subjective; the API exposes an all-genres resource.
