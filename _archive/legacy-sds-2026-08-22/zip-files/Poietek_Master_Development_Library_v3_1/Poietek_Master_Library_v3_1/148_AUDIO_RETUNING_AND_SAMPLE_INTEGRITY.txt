148 — AUDIO RETUNING & SAMPLE INTEGRITY
=======================================

A project can contain audio recorded or sampled at different references.

EXAMPLE

Instrument stem:
A440.

Session:
A432.

Poietek can detect approximate stable pitched content and report the reference
difference.

USER OPTIONS

Keep original.
Pitch-shift preview.
Render tuned copy.
Change project reference.
Mark source as intentionally detuned.

NON-DESTRUCTIVE

Original asset remains unchanged.

AudioClip stores tuning/pitch operation.

FORMANTS

For vocals/instruments, pitch shift can optionally preserve formants where the
selected algorithm supports it.

PERCUSSION

Do not force harmonic retuning onto unpitched/percussive sources.

AI asks when classification confidence is low.

END DOCUMENT 148
