IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY

A payment to an artist is generally a tip/gift/support payment unless it meets the
legal requirements for a charitable donation.

Do not market creator tips as tax-deductible donations.

RIGHTS-AWARE SUPPORT

Tip to one creator:
payment policy determines recipient.

Purchase collaborative song:
approved CommercePolicy determines sale allocations.

No rights claim should be inferred from a fan payment.

MOBILE STORE ROUTING

CommercePolicyRouter checks:
platform
storefront/territory
product
digital vs physical
tip/gift vs content purchase
current store rules.

On Apple platforms, creator content is treated as UGC/creator content and digital
purchases are subject to App Review payment rules; optional monetary gifts have a
separate rule when 100% goes to the receiver and are not connected to digital
content/services.

On Google Play, digital goods/services generally use Play Billing unless an
eligible current alternative-billing program applies; peer-to-peer/tax-exempt
donation categories are treated differently.

Never hard-code one global mobile checkout.
