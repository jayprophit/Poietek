POIETEK / STUDIO DAW STATION (SDS)
MASTER DEVELOPMENT LIBRARY — VERSION 1.0

Compiled from:
1) The complete product-development conversation supplied by the creator.
2) Deep review of GitHub repository: jayprophit/Studio-Daw-Station-SDS-
3) Current cross-platform/offline-first architecture recommendations.

WORKING PRODUCT NAME
Poietek is the working ecosystem name discussed in the design conversation.
Studio DAW Station (SDS) is the current repository/product label.
Final public branding should be chosen only after trademark/domain checks.

CORE RULE
One app. One project model. One creative graph. Many workspaces.
Offline first. Cloud optional. Hardware agnostic. Collaboration capable.
Professional depth without forcing beginners to see every advanced control.

LEGAL / CLEAN-ROOM RULE
Competitor products, patents, papers, hardware and interfaces may be studied for
functional principles, workflows and interoperability requirements. Do not copy
proprietary source code, protected assets, branded UI expression, undocumented
confidential protocols, or patented claims without appropriate legal review or
licensing. Compatibility layers should use documented/open interfaces wherever
possible.

===============================================================================

INTERNAL CREATOR COMMUNITY

PROFILE
bio, skills, genres, portfolio, public/private projects, follows, collaboration
availability, optional verified identity/equipment/region.

FEED
audio, video, image, project preview, template, device profile, tutorial, remix.
Interactions: like, comment, share, save, follow, report, remix/fork if allowed.

DIRECT MESSAGING
1:1, project/group chat, attachments, private previews, collaboration invites.
Include blocking, reporting, requests and spam controls.

COLLAB DISCOVERY
Search by role, genre, instrument, equipment, availability, language and portfolio.

CONTRIBUTOR FOOTPRINT
Audio and visual:
artist, vocalist, instrumentalist, producer, songwriter, composer, engineer,
mix/master, session musician, DJ, editor, director, camera, VFX, motion, graphic
design and business entities.

RECORDING WORKFLOW
Project setup can preload team.
When recording an unassigned track, prompt for performer, instrument/role,
engineer and session date. Guests can later claim profiles.

CONTRIBUTION IS NOT AUTOMATIC OWNERSHIP
AI can suggest a discussion split, but humans approve.

RIGHTS ENTITIES
People, labels, publishers, management, PRO/CMO, distributors, promoters,
visual-production entities and other contractual parties.

QUICK EXPORT
No ISRC/PRO requirement. Draft/private/master/stems according to permissions.

PUBLISH MODE
Optional validation for title, artists, writers, performers, producer, visual
credits, splits, labels/publishers, licences, identifiers, artwork and release
settings.

SAMPLE RIGHTS
Store source/licence. Before monetized/public release, flag unknown items and
offer attach/replace/seek-permission/original-alternative workflows. Do not claim
AI recreation makes an unlicensed protected work automatically safe.

BLOCKCHAIN
Optional content hash, timestamp, wallet and contract reference. Do not claim
blockchain replaces copyright/contract systems.

MONETISATION
Tips, direct sales, subscriptions/fan support, marketplace, commissions, services
and courses. Payment/KYC/tax/legal requirements are separate infrastructure.

CREATOR LICENCE SETTINGS
listen only, remix allowed, attribution, noncommercial, commercial available,
custom terms.

GOVERNANCE
Platform roadmap voting is separate from project legal approvals.

SHARING
Internal publishing is first-class. External platforms use connector-based
ShareService adapters and each platform's rules/APIs.

VISIBILITY
local only, private cloud, team, unlisted, community/followers, public.
Visibility and monetisation are separate choices.
