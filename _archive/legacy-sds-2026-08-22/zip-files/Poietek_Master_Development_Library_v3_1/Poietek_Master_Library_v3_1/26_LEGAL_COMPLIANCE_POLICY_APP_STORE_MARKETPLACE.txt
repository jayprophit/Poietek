POIETEK MASTER DEVELOPMENT LIBRARY — VERSION 1.6 ADDENDUM

26 — LEGAL, COMPLIANCE, POLICY, APP-STORE & MARKETPLACE ARCHITECTURE
===================================================================

PURPOSE

This volume identifies legal/compliance systems that engineering must support.
It is not legal advice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LEGAL DOMAINS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Intellectual property
Privacy/data protection
Consumer protection
Payments/tax
Marketplace/platform rules
Content moderation
Copyright notice/takedown
Music publishing/recording rights
AI/data/model licensing
Open-source licensing
App-store policies
Age/youth protection
Export/sanctions where applicable
Accessibility obligations where applicable

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TERMS / POLICIES REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLIC SERVICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Terms of Service
Privacy Notice
Cookie/Tracking Notice where applicable
Acceptable Use Policy
Community Guidelines
Marketplace Seller Terms
Creator Monetization Terms
Copyright/Takedown Policy
Repeat Infringer Policy where applicable
AI/Data Use Notice
Developer/SDK Terms
Storage Connector disclosure
Refund policy if commerce exists

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LOCAL-ONLY USERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A local-only user should not be forced to accept unrelated cloud/community terms
just to record audio locally, except software licence terms necessary for use.

Network/community features can require additional terms at activation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COPYRIGHT WORKFLOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Report content
Identify claimant
Identify work
Identify accused material
Status
Counter-notice/appeal workflow where legally appropriate
Preserve audit
Restrict content proportionately

Never make AI judge final infringement liability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SAMPLE / ASSET LICENSING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Store:
source
creator
licence
commercial permission
territory
expiry
attribution
proof/reference

Publish workflow surfaces missing evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. AI LICENSING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Track separately:
model code licence
model weights licence
dataset restrictions
provider terms
commercial-use status
output terms
attribution
remote-code risk

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. MARKETPLACE COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Seller identity
Payout eligibility
Tax information
Product licence
Refund/dispute
Malware review
Content rights
Version support
Moderation
Reporting

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PAYMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use established payment processors.
Do not store raw card details unless architecture/compliance deliberately assumes
that burden.

Ledger and processor records reconcile.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. APP STORE DISTRIBUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Maintain per-platform checklist:
bundle ID
signing
privacy disclosures
permissions
subscriptions/IAP rules
background behavior
file access
microphone/camera
user-generated content moderation
account deletion where required
export controls if applicable

Policies change; release process must verify current platform requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. AGE / COMMUNITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If minors are permitted:
age gates
DM safety
report/block
guardian/consent requirements where applicable
content restrictions
data minimization

Consider launching adult/general-age community first if youth-compliance burden
is not yet ready.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. PRIVACY ENGINEERING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data inventory.
Purpose.
Retention.
Export.
Delete.
Consent.
Processor/subprocessor register.
Regional hosting strategy later.

Project audio is not telemetry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. RIGHTS OF USERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Export account data.
Delete account data.
Disconnect providers.
Revoke sessions.
Delete remote replicas.
Retain local projects if user chooses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. LICENCE INVENTORY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every distributed:
library
font
icon
codec
model
preset
sample
IR
demo project

must have provenance/licence entry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. LEGAL HOLD / AUDIT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not build broad surveillance.
For legally required/auditable systems, isolate:
rights approvals
payments
moderation actions
security events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. APP FEATURE AVAILABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Some connectors/AI/publishing services may vary by country/provider.
Use capability/policy flags rather than hardcoded promises.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. ACCEPTANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LAW-001 local project works without community account.
LAW-002 account deletion workflow exists for hosted data.
LAW-003 provider token revocable.
LAW-004 public upload requires rights declaration where policy requires.
LAW-005 marketplace item stores licence.
LAW-006 copyrighted demo assets have distribution rights documented.
LAW-007 AI model has licence record.
LAW-008 open-source notices generated.
LAW-009 moderation action auditable.
LAW-010 payment ledger does not use float.
LAW-011 app-store permission disclosures generated/reviewed.
LAW-012 privacy export excludes other users' private data.
LAW-013 rights approvals cannot be silently rewritten.
LAW-014 legal policies versioned.
LAW-015 user can see terms version accepted.
