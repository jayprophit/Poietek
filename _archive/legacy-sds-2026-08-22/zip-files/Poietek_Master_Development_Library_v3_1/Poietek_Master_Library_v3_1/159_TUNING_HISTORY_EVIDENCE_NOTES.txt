159 — TUNING HISTORY & EVIDENCE NOTES
=====================================

CURRENT VERIFIED HISTORY

ISO 16:1975 specifies A=440 Hz and remains current after its 2022 review.

Historical research and contemporary 1939 reporting show that A440 emerged through
decades of international negotiation and an International Standards Association
conference held at BBC Broadcasting House in London on 11–12 May 1939.

Before standardization, pitch varied materially between locations, periods and
ensembles.

PRODUCT POLICY

Poietek does not encode unsupported claims that one family, individual or secret
group recently changed the musical standard.

CREATIVE FREEDOM

A415, custom pitch or another temperament in Poietek.
