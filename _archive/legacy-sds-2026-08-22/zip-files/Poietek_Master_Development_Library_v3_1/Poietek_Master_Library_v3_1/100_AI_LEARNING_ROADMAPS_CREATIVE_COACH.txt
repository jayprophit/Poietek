AI LEARNING ASSISTANT

Can:
explain current control;
answer "why";
diagnose a project issue;
suggest next lesson;
generate a practice exercise;
highlight relevant controls;
compare before/after;
quiz;
review a mix/project against a learning rubric;
build a personalized roadmap.

Must not:
secretly rewrite project;
pretend subjective advice is a fact;
force cloud AI;
upload private media without permission.

SHOW ME MODE

Assistant can:
focus/highlight UI;
open a disposable tutorial branch;
perform preview commands;
let learner accept/reject.

LEARNER PROJECTS

Tutorial projects are forkable.
User keeps what they make.
Progress is portable.

OFFLINE

Optional local model.
