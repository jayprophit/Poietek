POIETEK MASTER DEVELOPMENT LIBRARY — VERSION 2.3

97 — DAO, DEFI, NFT, ORDINALS, RUNES & OP_CAT CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

DAO
ADAPT LATER.

Possible:
community grants;
open-source roadmap voting;
extension/device-profile stewardship;
creator collective treasury proposals.

Never use token votes to override:
copyright contracts;
contributor approvals;
privacy rights;
legal obligations;
individual payout entitlements.

DEFI
RESEARCH ONLY.

No lending/yield/liquidity-pool system belongs in the core creative app.
Any future financial protocol requires separate legal/security/product review.

NFT / DIGITAL COLLECTIBLE
OPTIONAL.

Possible:
collectible edition;
access pass;
event ticket;
proof-linked digital edition;
license receipt.

Token ownership never automatically equals copyright ownership.

BITCOIN ORDINAL INSCRIPTIONS
RESEARCH/OPTIONAL EXPORT.

Inscriptions can hold arbitrary content/metadata as Bitcoin-native digital
artifacts. Poietek may someday export an approved public collectible/provenance
artifact, but should not put private Rights Manifests or large studio masters
on-chain by default.

RUNES / RUNESTONES
RESEARCH ONLY.

Runes are fungible Bitcoin-native digital commodities with protocol messages
encoded as runestones. Possible future community/collectible experiments are
separate from royalties and legal rights.

OP_CAT
DO NOT DEPEND ON IT.

BIP 347 proposes OP_CAT in Tapscript, but current Bitcoin Core still treats OP_CAT
as disabled. Poietek must not build product-critical functionality on an
unactivated consensus proposal.

ETFs
REJECT FROM CORE.

An exchange-traded fund is a regulated investment product, not a creator-app
feature. If Poietek ever enters investment/securities products it is a separate
regulated business.

END DOCUMENT 97
