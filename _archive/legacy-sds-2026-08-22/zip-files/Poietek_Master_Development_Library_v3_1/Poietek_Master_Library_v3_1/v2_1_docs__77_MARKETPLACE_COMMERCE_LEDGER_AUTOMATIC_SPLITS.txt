AUTOMATIC SPLIT

Use approved CommercePolicy.

Example only:
Creator A 5000 bps
Creator B 2500 bps
Company C 1500 bps
Platform 1000 bps.

Actual splits come from approved agreement/policy, never AI inference.

INTEGER MONEY

All ledger calculations use minor units and deterministic rounding.

PAYMENT PROCESSOR

A marketplace rail such as Stripe Connect can support splitting a platform charge
into transfers to multiple connected accounts.

Still required:
seller onboarding;
identity/KYC as required by processor/law;
payout account;
refund/chargeback reconciliation;
tax handling;
territory availability.

HISTORICAL IMMUTABILITY

Order stores CommercePolicy version.
Changing tomorrow's split does not rewrite yesterday's sale.

DISPUTE

Freeze affected unpaid allocation.
Do not delete ledger history.
Resolve through adjustment entries.

ACCEPTANCE
SELL-001 shares total exactly 10000 bps.
SELL-002 £/$0.01 rounding deterministic.
SELL-003 refund creates reversal/adjustment.
SELL-004 old sale retains old policy.
SELL-005 missing payout account holds beneficiary balance rather than paying wrong person.
