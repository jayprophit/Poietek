161 — COMMUNITY PLAYER TUNING ARCHITECTURE
==========================================

CLARIFIED PRODUCT REQUIREMENT

Poietek's community surfaces can play a work using its creator tuning or an
optional listener/community playback tuning.

SURFACES

Hub video player
audio player
community wall
creator channel
archive
feed
playlist
remix pages
learning pages
live/replay pages.

CANONICAL MASTER

Never changes.

Example:
Creator master = A432.

Community player can:
play original A432
or
offer optional alternate playback.

A listener preference can be:
Original
432
440
442
Custom.

The player displays the active mode.

PLAYBACK RETUNING IS A DERIVATIVE EXPERIENCE

It is not:
a new rights owner
a new canonical master
a society registration change
a replacement project.

END DOCUMENT 161
