153 — ALTERNATIVE TUNING CREATIVE RIGHTS POLICY
===============================================

CREATOR CONTROL

A project may use:
A432
A415
A442
custom reference
just intonation
historical temperament
microtonal tuning
adaptive tuning.

Poietek does not silently convert it to A440.

The tuning choice is part of the creative/project metadata.

PROJECT OWNER can choose:
Preserve Original
Compatibility Advisory
Strict Destination.

A collaborator may propose another tuning but cannot overwrite the project
reference without permission.
