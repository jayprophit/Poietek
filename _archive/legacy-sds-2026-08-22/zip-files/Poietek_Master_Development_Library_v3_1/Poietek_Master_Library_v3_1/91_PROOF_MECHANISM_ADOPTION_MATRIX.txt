Poietek separates NETWORK CONSENSUS from APPLICATION PROOFS.

Network mechanisms such as PoW, PoS, DPoS, PoA, PoET, Proof-of-Space,
PBFT/dBFT/FBA and similar protocols are relevant only if Poietek evaluates an
external chain or future trusted consortium network.

Poietek should not build a custom blockchain just to claim use of these terms.

Application proofs are directly useful:
Proof of Contribution
Proof of Consent
Proof of Identity
Proof of Data Integrity
Proof of Transaction History
Proof of Interoperability
Proof of Chaos/Resilience
Proof of Concept.

Adapt carefully:
Reputation
Impact
Engagement
Discovery
Location
Legacy
Environmental Impact
Customization
Trust.

A non-standard phrase is never used as a security primitive until a precise,
reviewed protocol is selected.
