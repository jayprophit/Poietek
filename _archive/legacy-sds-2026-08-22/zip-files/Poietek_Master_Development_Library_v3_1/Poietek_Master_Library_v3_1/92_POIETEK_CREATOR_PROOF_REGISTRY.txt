92 — CREATOR PROOF REGISTRY
===========================

COMPANIONS
schemas/proof-record.schema.v1.json
packages/proofs/src/ProofService.ts

PROOF OF CONTRIBUTION
Party + project + session + role + track/clip/object + timestamp + content/version
hash + approval/signature where appropriate.

Contribution does not automatically equal ownership.

PROOF OF CONSENT
Binds a Party to a precise Rights Manifest/version/policy scope.

PROOF OF IDENTITY
Uses Contributor Passport plus provider/KYC/passkey/ZK verification where useful.
Raw identity documents remain private.

PROOF OF DATA INTEGRITY
SHA-256, signatures, C2PA, Merkle roots and immutable audit records.

PROOF OF TRANSACTION HISTORY
Order + Commerce Policy version + ledger + processor receipt + settlement proof.

PROOF OF INTEROPERABILITY
Automated evidence that a project/file/provider round-trips without critical loss.

PROOF OF CHAOS
Evidence from deliberate network/provider/storage/worker/payment failure tests.

PROOF OF TRUST
A transparent bundle of underlying evidence.
Never a mysterious social-credit score controlling rights or money.

END DOCUMENT 92
