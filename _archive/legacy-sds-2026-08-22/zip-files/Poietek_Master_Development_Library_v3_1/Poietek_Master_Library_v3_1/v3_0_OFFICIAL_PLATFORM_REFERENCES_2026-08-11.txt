Official/current sources reviewed 11 August 2026:

YouTube Help — Recommended upload encoding settings
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171
Current recommendations include MP4/H.264, AAC-LC/Opus/Eclipsa audio, 48 kHz
audio sample rate, native frame rate and BT.709 for SDR. No A440 tuning-reference
requirement was found on the page.

YouTube Help — Encoding specifications for music videos
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6039860
Current partner specification recommends FLAC or Linear PCM, 48 kHz recommended,
24-bit recommended, stereo (plus current immersive options). No A440 tuning
requirement was found on the page.

YouTube Help — Live encoder settings
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2853702
Current live delivery requirements focus on codecs, bitrate, sample rate, frame
rate, keyframes and colour space.

EBU R128:
https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128
Broadcast loudness profile remains a separate technical delivery concern from
musical tuning reference.
