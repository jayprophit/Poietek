115 — AI RECOMMENDATIONS, SAFETY & AUTO-FIX
===========================================

AI ROLE

Translate measurements into useful production advice.

EXAMPLE

Instead of:
"True Peak: -0.1 dBTP."

Assistant:
"Your master is very close to full scale. Spotify's current guidance asks for
more true-peak margin; preview a limiter ceiling change before export."

ACTION MODES

ADVICE ONLY
No mutation.

PREVIEWABLE COMMAND
Build non-destructive project command.

MANUAL HARDWARE ACTION
"Lower microphone preamp."

REQUIRES MORE MEASUREMENT
"Play the loudest chorus first."

NO SILENT MASTERING

AI may not automatically:
change preamp
enable phantom
insert compressor
EQ vocal
limit master
normalize
change sample rate

without policy/user approval.

AUTO MODE

User may enable bounded helpers:
Prevent digital master overload.
Record safety track.
Auto input trim on verified digital preamp.
Delivery conformity pass.

Every automatic action:
bounded
logged
undoable when technically possible
visible.

END DOCUMENT 115
