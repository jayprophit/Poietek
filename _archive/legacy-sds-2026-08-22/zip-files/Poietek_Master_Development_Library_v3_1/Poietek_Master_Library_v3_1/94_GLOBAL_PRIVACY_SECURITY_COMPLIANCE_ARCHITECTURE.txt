CORE PRODUCT RULE
Creative media is not analytics.
Rights/contracts are not marketing data.
Payment/KYC data is isolated from public/social data.

GDPR/UK STYLE
privacy by design, transparency, minimization, access/correction/deletion and
portable user-data workflows where scope requires.

CCPA/CPRA STYLE
notice, know/delete/correct, opt-out of sale/sharing and sensitive-information
controls when applicable.

OTHER JURISDICTIONS
Colorado, Utah, China PIPL, Singapore PDPA and Australian Privacy Act/APP modules
are enabled according to actual scope and legal review.

SECURITY FRAMEWORK
Adopt NIST CSF 2.0 now for governance/risk structure.

SECURE DEVELOPMENT
Use NIST SSDF 1.1 as current final baseline while monitoring the draft 1.2 update.

AI GOVERNANCE
Map AI systems to NIST AI RMF and GenAI Profile.

PAYMENTS
Minimize PCI DSS scope by keeping raw cardholder data with compliant payment rails.

ASSURANCE TARGETS
ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 are future hosted-service assurance targets, not immediate
claims.

HIPAA/GLBA
Conditional only if Poietek enters those regulated scopes.
